Business Question:
"Which benefit programs have the highest improper-payment exposure among flagged payments,
and what is the projected recovery if those payments are investigated rather than released?"

Context:
The Sentinel agency needs to prioritize investigative resources across five benefit programs
(TANF, SNAP, Child Care, Disability, Veteran's). This query uses the synced UC table
(gold_open_queue, synced into Lakebase as sentinel.synced_gold_open_queue) to rank programs
by total exposure and projected recovery, helping the Deputy Commissioner allocate examiner
capacity where the return on investigation is highest.
